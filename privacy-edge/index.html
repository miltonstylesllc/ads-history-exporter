Amazon Ads History Exporter — Privacy Policy

Milton Styles LLC  ·  Effective November 2, 2026

The bottom line Your Amazon data never leaves your browser: the extension saves it as a CSV on your computer and we never receive it. We only receive an email address if you subscribe to Pro, log in to it, or choose to leave one on the removal feedback page.

What This Extension Does

Amazon Ads History Exporter is a Microsoft Edge extension that reads the History data already shown in your Amazon Ads console (campaign, ad group or whole account) and saves it as CSV files on your computer. Pro adds Batch Export across campaigns. All of this runs locally in your browser.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Your Amazon advertising data or the files you export
  • Your Amazon login, password, session tokens or cookies
  • Your browsing history
  • Analytics, crash reports or usage tracking of any kind

What Stays in Your Browser

The extension keeps a few settings and status values (for example, whether an export is running and your Batch Export mode) in your browser's local storage. ExtensionPay, our payment service, keeps the install date and, once you open the upgrade or login page, its key for your browser and your subscription record in your browser's sync storage, which Edge can sync to your Microsoft account. We don't receive these browser copies; the email ExtensionPay shares with us is covered below.

Your Email Address

You can use the free version without giving us an email. We receive an email address in two cases only:

  • Pro. If you subscribe or log in, you enter your email on ExtensionPay's checkout or login page, and ExtensionPay shares it with us. The extension also keeps your subscription record, including that email, in your browser's sync storage.
  • Removal feedback. If you choose to leave an email on the feedback page, we use it only to reply to you.

We use a Pro email to run your subscription: confirming it, a one-time welcome email, and replies when you write to us. The welcome email may mention our other tools. Beyond that, we send news about new features or products only if you ask for it (reply “news”), and you can stop it at any time by replying “unsubscribe”. We never sell or rent your email, never give it to advertising platforms, and never use it for ads. To have us delete it, write to support@miltonstyles.com and we will remove it from our records. ExtensionPay and Stripe may keep their own records, such as billing records the law requires.

Third Parties

  • ExtensionPay and Stripe (Pro only) handle checkout and payment. Card details go to Stripe and are never visible to us. When the extension loads, it checks your subscription status with extensionpay.com; no Amazon data is included. To use Pro on another computer, log in with the same email.
  • Google Forms. When you remove the extension, the browser opens an optional feedback page; Google Forms receives only the answers you submit. The page address includes the extension version, that the browser is Edge, and whether Pro was active.
