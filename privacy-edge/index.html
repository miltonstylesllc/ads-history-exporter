Amazon Ads History Exporter — Privacy Policy

Milton Styles LLC  ·  Effective October 20, 2026

The bottom line Your advertising data never leaves your browser: the extension saves it as a CSV on your computer and we never receive it. The only things sent anywhere are the Pro subscription check with ExtensionPay and, if you remove the extension, the optional feedback page described below.

What This Extension Does

Amazon Ads History Exporter is a Microsoft Edge extension that reads the History data already displayed in your Amazon Ads and saves it as CSV files on your computer. This can include campaign-level History, ad-group-level History, and account-level History.

When you click "Export to CSV," the extension scrolls through the full list on screen, captures every row, and triggers a download directly to your device. No data is sent anywhere else.

For Pro users, the extension can also run a user-initiated Batch Export from Campaign Manager to export multiple campaign histories in sequence. This processing still happens locally in your browser.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect any personal information, unless you choose to leave an email address on the feedback page
  • We do not collect your Amazon account credentials, username, or password
  • We do not collect your Amazon session tokens or cookies
  • We do not record or store your browsing history
  • We do not collect or store the advertising data you export
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind

How Your Data Is Handled

All processing happens locally inside your browser. The extension reads what is already visible on your screen, assembles it into a CSV file, and downloads it to your computer using your browser's standard file download.

The extension saves a small amount of status data and preferences in your browser's local storage — for example, whether an export is currently running, your Batch Export mode, and how many exports you have run (used to time a one-off review request). This data never leaves your device. Running-export status ends with the browser session; preferences and counts stay until you remove the extension.

The CSV file produced is downloaded directly to your computer. Milton Styles LLC never receives, sees, or stores this file.

Permissions Explained

The extension requests only the minimum permissions needed to function:

PermissionWhy it is needed
activeTab To read the Change History table on the Amazon Ads tab you have open
downloads To trigger the CSV file download to your computer
storage To store export state and preferences locally in your browser
Hosts: advertising.amazon.com, advertising.amazon.ca, advertising.amazon.in To limit the extension to supported Amazon Advertising Console hosts (US, Canada, and India) only

Third Parties

Your advertising data is never shared with any third party. The extension has no backend servers and no analytics service.

Payment processing (Pro tier only): If you subscribe to the Pro tier, payment processing is handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) using Stripe. ExtensionPay uses a random, non-personally-identifiable identifier that it assigns when the extension is installed — not your Amazon account — to verify subscription status. If you already subscribe to Pro on another computer or browser, log in with the same email in the extension to restore it; it is the same subscription, not a second purchase. Stripe collects and processes your payment information directly. Full payment method details — including your full card number and security code — are handled exclusively by Stripe and are never visible to Milton Styles LLC. Your name, card information, and payment data are subject to the privacy policies of ExtensionPay and Stripe.

Subscription status check: When the extension loads, it contacts extensionpay.com to verify your Pro subscription status. No advertising data, Amazon account information, or browsing history is included in this request.

Feedback When You Remove the Extension

When you remove the extension, Edge opens a short optional feedback page on our site. The page address includes the extension version, that the browser is Edge, and whether Pro was active. Answering is optional; we receive only what you choose to submit, including an email address if you choose to leave one. Answers are collected through Google Forms and used only to improve the extension and to reply if you asked us to.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the extension's Edge Add-ons update log.
