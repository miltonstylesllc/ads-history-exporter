Questions about exporting Amazon Ads change history

Updated October 22, 2026

How do I export Amazon Ads change history to CSV?

Install Amazon Ads History Exporter from the Chrome Web Store, open the History tab of any campaign or ad group in Amazon Ads, set the date range you want, and click Export to CSV in the extension popup. The extension scrolls the whole History list, pages through every page, and downloads a CSV with the change type, the old value, the new value, and the date and time of each change.

Is it free?

Yes. The free version exports any campaign or ad-group History tab with no limit on the number of exports. Pro ($9.99 a month or $79 a year) adds Batch Export across many campaigns, account-level History export, date presets including a max date range, and an Enriched CSV with campaign name, ad group, campaign type, and URL columns.

How far back can I export Amazon Ads change history?

As far back as Amazon's History tab allows. The tab's own date presets stop at the start of the year, and dragging its calendar all the way back reaches about 12 months. Amazon's Lifetime date setting, chosen on another Amazon Ads page, carries over to the History tab and may reach further: up to about 13 months in our latest testing, varying by campaign.

Pro's max date range applies that Lifetime carry-over for you on any campaign or ad-group History tab, and Batch Export allows it for every campaign in one run. Amazon has been narrowing this window over time, so exporting now is the safest way to keep your own record.

I already use the Amazon Ads API. Why would I use this extension?

The Amazon Ads API's change history covers a rolling 90 days for Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands, and it requires API developer access. The extension exports as far back as the History tab in the Amazon Ads console allows, about 13 months in our latest testing, with no developer account or code. That makes it the practical way to backfill older change history.

Does my advertising data leave my computer?

No. The extension runs entirely in your browser and saves the CSV straight to your computer. It does not collect, store, or send your advertising data, and it never reads your Amazon password or session.

Can I export change history for every campaign at once?

Yes, with Pro. Batch Export walks through every campaign, or only the ones you select, and exports each History tab at the widest date range available, with no row limit; very large exports are split into numbered files. That is the way to backfill a whole account.

Account-Level History exports every change across your advertising account into one CSV, but Amazon caps that table at 10,000 rows, which on an active account can cover only a few weeks. Use it for weekly or monthly upkeep after the backfill, not for the backfill itself.

Which Amazon Ads marketplaces are supported?

US (advertising.amazon.com), Canada (advertising.amazon.ca), and India (advertising.amazon.in). We add marketplaces based on demand; email support@miltonstyles.com to request yours.

What if the extension doesn't work as expected?

Amazon updates the Amazon Ads console often, and sometimes a change affects a step the extension relies on. We test regularly and fix these quickly, but Amazon may have changed something we haven't caught yet.

First, refresh the Amazon Ads tab and try again; a tab opened before the extension was installed or updated needs one refresh. If it still doesn't work, email support@miltonstyles.com with the page you were on, what you clicked, and any message you saw. We'll look into it and let you know when it's fixed.
